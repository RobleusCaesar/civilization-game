Guide

Welcome to the valley

A campfire, a handful of villagers, a wild green valley — and a rival chief across the map who wants all of it.

Drag to look around, pinch to zoom, tap to act.

With a mouse, drag a box round your people to take them together, and right-drag to look around. That's every control in the game.

Raise your village

Tap a building, tap the ground — your villagers do the rest.

Everything grows from the Town Center. Raise it through three levels and each one unlocks new buildings, new soldiers, new tricks.

Lay roads to your buildings: one a road touches works 10% better, and says so in gold.

Gather everything

Tap a villager, then a forest, a hill or a gold seam — off they go. Wood builds, stone fortifies, gold pays for the sharp stuff.

Every valley is short on one resource, and your scouts name it on day one. Claim it before the rival does.

Feed the tribe

When the stores run dry, soldiers desert — villagers keep working, so the town can farm its way back. Plant farms by fertile soil, hunt the woods, fish where the fish jump.

Station a villager at a farm, lodge or quarry and it works all day — a fed village runs itself.

Choose your Origin

Every game deals you three Origin cards — keep one. It's the gift your people start with: faster axes, braver spears, deeper purses.

Your rival drafts one too. On Hard you won't know which…

The wilds bite back

Wolves hunt the pines, bears own the deep forest, and boars charge anyone who steps too close.

Barbarian tribes — the Wolfskins, the Woadkin, the Sea Folk — land in boats, burn what they find, and leave. They raid your rival just as happily.

Raise an army

Defenders from the Barracks, archers from the Range, riders from the Stable, engines from the Siege Workshop. A new Barracks or Range sends its first soldier the day it stands.

Higher halls train fiercer troops — up to the Bronze Champion. Spears, bows and horses each cover another's weakness. Mix them.

March as one

Muster your soldiers into an army under one banner — tap the banner and the whole host answers as one.

Sound the horn to call every idle blade to a spot. And when raiders reach the walls, raise the levy — the whole village takes up arms.

Man your towers

A watchtower shoots nothing by itself. Train archers at the Range, select them and tap the tower: two climb up, see twice as far and shoot everything in sight.

Walls turn raiders and gates let your own people through. Villagers shelter in the Town Center when trouble knocks.

Rule the water

The Dock floats fishing boats, transport rafts for your soldiers, fire warships and bombard ships that break coasts.

Some valleys are islands, and the whole war crosses by sea. Old sailors swear something vast moves under open water…

Know your rival

Across the valley a rival chief builds, schemes and remembers. Every game they roll a different temperament — hoarder, raider, sea-wolf.

Scout them early and watch how they probe your defenses. They are certainly watching yours.

Two roads to glory

Break the rival's Town Center and the valley is yours.

Or out-build them entirely: raise an Ancient Wonder — one of ten monuments — and win without a war. Either road ends on the leaderboard.
