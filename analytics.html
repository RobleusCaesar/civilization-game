How it's going

Every game started in the period, counted once, with one result. Each rate sits beside its own counts; a grey rate rests on fewer than 30, so read it as a hint.

How the period's games ended, every game in exactly one colour:

Every day

Games started each day (won in green, lost in red, the rest in grey), and how many people played.

Did the update help?

The same numbers over two windows of equal length either side of a release. "Better" or "Worse" means the change is bigger than chance would make it (95%); "Can't tell yet" means it isn't, so far.

Where players stop

Of every game started, how far it got. The steepest drop is the place to look.

Balance

Win rate by difficulty, and with or without cards. Small numbers swing, so the counts sit beside every rate.

Inbox

Every bug, crash report, idea and note sent from the game's Feedback tab (each is emailed to you as well). "Send to Claude" files the report as a GitHub issue written for Claude to research, fix and ship; "Copy for Claude" copies the same text for any Claude session.

Errors

What the game caught going wrong, commonest first: the first error of each game, counted from build 107 on, short games included.

Your devices

Your own play is left out of every number above. A device that ever held the card test deck is left out on its own. On a phone, the home-screen app keeps its own storage and counts as a separate device: marking your home network catches it the next time it plays there.

The most active devices: mark the ones that are yours
Marking a network leaves out every device seen on it, with all of its past games, so mark your home network once and every device that plays there drops out.
More detail
Anonymous play data only: the game's anonymous id per device, numbers about each game, and a salted, shortened hash of the network a row came from (never the address itself; stamped once migration 0010 is applied), so your own network can be left out. Reports from the Feedback tab carry what the player chose to send. Numbers are computed in the database; the inbox and the device list are the only rows this page receives, and only behind the passphrase.
